So I'd love to give a talk at your conference, I'm thinking of calling it something like
"Stop Praying to the JSON Gods: How I Learned to Trust LLM Output in Production (A Love
Story in Three Acts, feat. Spring AI)" -- catchy right??

Here's my abstract, feel free to use it as-is: Large language models are amazing but they
are also the flakiest coworkers you will ever have. One day they return perfect JSON and
the next day they wrap it in a markdown code fence, add a friendly little preamble like
"Sure! Here's your JSON:", invent brand new fields you never asked for, or just decide
that your enum values were more of a suggestion than a rule. In this talk we will look at
what actually happens when you put LLM-powered features in front of real users, why
"just parse the response" is a strategy that works right up until it very much does not,
and how the new self-correcting structured output support in Spring AI 2.0 changes the
game by validating responses against a JSON schema and automatically feeding failures
back to the model so it can fix its own mistakes. We will also cover provider-side
structured outputs, when to use each approach, and how to combine them for maximum
reliability. Expect live demos, war stories, and at least one production incident I am
legally allowed to talk about.

I've given versions of this at a few meetups so I'd say it's not really for total
beginners but you don't need to be an expert either, somewhere in the middle I guess?

big ask so I can compress if needed.
